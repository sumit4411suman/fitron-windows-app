Privacy notice

Last updated {{ set.pv.updated }}

Who we are

{{ gymName }} is the Data Fiduciary for member data. Fitron Gym Accounting Solution processes that data on the gym’s behalf as a Data Processor.

What we collect

Name, phone, WhatsApp number, email, date of birth, gender, address, emergency contact, photo, ID and address proof, health notes you choose to share, membership and payment records, attendance, and face or fingerprint templates if you use biometric entry.

Why we use it

To register and identify you, run your membership, take payments and issue GST invoices, send reminders and receipts on WhatsApp, control door access, and keep you safe in the gym. We don’t sell your data or use it for unrelated purposes.

Your consent

We process your data with your consent, given at registration, or where the law allows it without consent (for example, keeping tax records). You can withdraw consent at any time; this stops future processing but may mean we can’t continue your membership.

Your rights

You can ask for a summary of your data, ask us to correct or complete it, ask us to erase it once it is no longer needed, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance. Contact the Grievance Officer below. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

For members under 18 we need verifiable consent from a parent or guardian, and we don’t track or target children with advertising.

How long we keep it

While your membership is active, then for the period set by the gym, after which personal details are erased. Invoices and payment records are kept as required by tax law, linked only to your member ID.

Security and breaches

Data is stored encrypted with role-based access; staff only see what their role needs. If a breach happens we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Sharing

Only with service providers who help run the gym (payment gateway, WhatsApp provider, cloud hosting, biometric device vendor), under contracts that protect your data.

Cookie and storage notice

Fitron uses essential browser storage to keep you signed in, remember your branch and theme, and save your work. These are needed for the app to work. Optional analytics storage is used only if you allow it in the banner, and you can change that choice at any time.

Linked WhatsApp
{{ set.waLink.number }} · Connected{{ set.waLink.meta }}
Not linked · no API key needed, just scan a QR code
Works like WhatsApp Web: the Fitron connector (a small app on the gym computer or our server) stays linked to your WhatsApp and sends reminders, invoices and renewals by itself at the scheduled time. It sends one message every 8 to 15 seconds, up to 250 a day, to keep your number safe.
This is unofficial automation of WhatsApp. WhatsApp can restrict numbers that send too many messages to people who haven’t saved your number. Use it for your own members only, never cold broadcasts, and keep a backup number.

Click-to-chat works today with the gym’s own WhatsApp on this phone or computer: every send opens a chat with the member’s number and the message filled in, and you press Send. Bulk reminders run as a queue, one tap each. Fully automatic sending (no tap, scheduled at 7 am) needs the Business API details below, which a developer connects in the production build.

Messages go through the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API using Meta-approved templates. In this demo, sending is simulated: each message moves from Sent to Delivered to Read within a few seconds, and numbers that aren’t 10 digits fail and are logged.
