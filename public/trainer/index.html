Before we begin

What we collect, and why

Fitron is a health app, so most of what we hold is about your body and your habits. Here it is in plain language. Nothing starts until you agree.

What we collect and what we do with it

  • Your name, email or phone to create your account and sign you in.
  • Your age, sex, height, weight, injuries and goal to work out your calorie and protein targets and build your training split.
  • Your diet, allergies, city and food budget to suggest meals sold near you that you can actually eat.
  • Your workouts, water, steps, habits and streaks to show your progress over time.
  • What you say to the AI Coach, sent to an AI provider so it can answer you. We do not send your name or email with it.
  • Your payment status from the payment gateway. We never see your card or UPI details.
