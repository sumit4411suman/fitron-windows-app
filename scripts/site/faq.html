AI Trainer

Is FITRON available in India?

Yes. Both products are live across India, with prices in rupees and payment by UPI, card or net banking. Start the AI trainer with a free trial, and gym owners can open Gym Accounting today.

Do I need a gym to use the AI trainer?

No. Tell FITRON what equipment you have, whether that's a full gym or a pair of dumbbells at home, and your plan is built around it. More on the AI trainer.

Is the AI trainer medical advice?

No. FITRON gives general fitness and nutrition guidance, and AI replies can be wrong. For pain, an injury or a health condition, please see a doctor or physio.

Gym Accounting

How does Gym Accounting work?

You set up your plans and members, then record each sale or renewal. FITRON makes numbered invoices, tracks payments and outstanding dues, and keeps your expenses and profit & loss in one console. Start with the 7-day free trial, then pick Starter, Professional or Enterprise. More on gym accounting.

Does Gym Accounting handle GST and my accountant's books?

Yes. Every sale gets a numbered invoice. When GST is switched on, it shows your GSTIN and the CGST and SGST split (or IGST) at the rate you set. At month-end you export receipts, expenses and ledgers as Excel or CSV files for your accountant. How GST billing works.

Can I export my data to Excel?

Yes. Reports, ledgers and accounting records export as Excel or CSV files on the Professional and Enterprise plans, and every invoice can be downloaded as a PDF.

Does gym accounting work for more than one branch?

Yes. Multi-branch management comes with the Enterprise plan (₹3,999 a month), with branch-wise and consolidated reports. Extra branches are ₹499 a month. More on gym accounting.

Pricing, trials and billing

How much does FITRON cost?

The AI Trainer is ₹299 a month (AI Pro) or ₹499 a month (AI Premium). Gym Accounting is ₹999 (Starter), ₹1,999 (Professional) or ₹3,999 (Enterprise) a month. Prices include GST, and yearly plans cost less than twelve months. See all plans.

How long is the free trial?

7 days on both products, no card needed. Gym Accounting shows the days left at the top of the console; when the trial ends your data stays as it was and you pay online to continue (UPI AutoPay, card or net banking).

How do I cancel?

Plans renew automatically through Razorpay (UPI AutoPay, card or net-banking mandate) for the period you chose, monthly or yearly. You can cancel any time from Settings › Plan & billing in Gym Accounting, or Settings › Subscription in the AI Trainer. The plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. Partnership

How does the gym partnership work?

Pick a partner plan: Referral (₹999 a month), Software with Gym Accounting Professional (₹1,999) or Enterprise with multi-branch accounting (₹3,999). You promote the AI Trainer to your members and earn 70% of every eligible subscription, settled monthly. See partner plans.

How does the 70% revenue share work?

A partner gym receives 70% of the eligible AI Trainer subscription revenue attributed to it. It is worked out on the price before GST, after refunds, chargebacks and agreed payment-processing deductions, and settled monthly, subject to verification and your signed partnership agreement. It is not guaranteed income: it depends on how many of your members subscribe. The examples in the pricing section are illustrative.

Privacy

What happens to my data?

You're in control. FITRON follows India's DPDP Act, 2023: consent first, data hosted in India, export or delete any time, and members' AI conversations are never shown to the gym. The AI replies are written by an AI provider that may process them outside India: the privacy policy says what is sent. Read the policies.
