How it works

Your members train. Your gym earns, every month.

Members link the FITRON AI Trainer to your gym with your code. Whenever they pay for it, 70% of the price before GST is your gym's, for as long as they stay on a plan.

  1. 1

    Share your gym code

    On WhatsApp, at the front desk or in your gym group. Every partner gym gets its own code and link.

  2. 2

    Members train free for 7 days

    They are linked to your gym the moment they enter the code. Nothing to pay for you or for them.

  3. 3

    They pick a plan

    AI Pro or AI Premium, monthly or yearly, paid straight to FITRON by UPI or card. You never collect money.

  4. 4

    You earn 70%, every month

    Not once, but on every payment while they stay linked, settled to your account monthly with a statement in your console.
